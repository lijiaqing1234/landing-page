I submitted a manuscript on gradient-free post-training for large language
models to **AAAI 2027**.

The work compares Evolution Strategies with Group Relative Policy Optimization
and studies reasoning-trajectory diversity and pass@k behavior. The broader
goal is to understand when gradient-free optimization offers practical
advantages for LLM post-training.
