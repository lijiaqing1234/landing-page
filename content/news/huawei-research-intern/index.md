I joined **Huawei Noah's Ark Lab** as a Research Intern and began contributing
to **LLM4AD-NEXT**, an open-source agent platform for LLM-driven automated
algorithm design and evolutionary optimization.

My work focuses on developing and refining extensible end-to-end workflows,
with an emphasis on configurability, evaluation, comparability, and integration
across automated heuristic-design methods.
