**LLM4AD-NEXT** is an open-source agent platform for LLM-driven automated
algorithm design and evolutionary optimization.

I contribute to the ongoing development of extensible end-to-end workflows
that integrate multiple automated heuristic-design methods. My work emphasizes
configurability, evaluation, comparability, and extensibility across the
platform.
