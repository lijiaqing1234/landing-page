This project investigates gradient-free optimization for large language model
post-training by comparing **Evolution Strategies** with **Group Relative
Policy Optimization (GRPO)**.

The study focuses on reasoning-trajectory diversity and pass@k behavior, with
the goal of identifying settings in which gradient-free optimization provides
practical advantages. A related manuscript has been submitted to **AAAI 2027**.
